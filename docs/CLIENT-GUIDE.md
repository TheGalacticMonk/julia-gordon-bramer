# Updating your website

A short guide to changing the words on your website, and adding essays, books and events.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **your website's address followed by /admin** (for example `juliagordonbramer.com/admin`).
2. Type your email and password and press **Log in**.

You'll see your **home screen**. It has big buttons for the things you'll do most:

- **Edit a page:** Home, Tarot, Decoding Sylvia Plath, Books, Events and Contact.
- **Add to your website:** a new essay, a new book, or a new upcoming event.
- **Quotes:** your "In the Press" quotes.

You can always get back to the home screen by clicking the star at the top left.

---

## Changing the words on a page

1. On your home screen, click the page you want, for example **Edit Tarot**.
2. The page opens in two halves. **On the left are the words you can change.** On the right is a **preview** of how the page will look.
3. Click into any box and type.

**Your work saves itself as you type.** The preview on the right updates within a moment. Nobody else can see these changes yet.

When you're happy, press **Publish changes** (top right). Your website updates within a few seconds.

> **Tip:** The small grey text under each box explains what it's for and where it appears on the site.

**Longer text** (like the introduction on the Home page) has a small toolbar: **B** for bold, *I* for italic, and a link button. To make a link, select some words first, click the link button and paste the web address.

**Emptied a box by mistake?** If you leave a box empty, the original wording comes back on the website.

---

## Adding an essay (Decoding Sylvia Plath)

1. On your home screen, click **Add an essay**.
2. Type the **title**.
3. Add a **picture**. It always shows in full: nothing gets cropped.
4. Type or paste the **essay text** into the big box.
5. Check the preview, then press **Publish changes**.

**Changing the order:** click **See all essays or reorder**, then drag an essay by the handle on its left. The essay list on the website follows the same order.

**Editing an essay later:** click **See all essays**, click the essay, make your changes and press **Publish changes**.

---

## Adding a book

1. Click **Add a book**.
2. Fill in the title, the details and the cover picture.
3. Add "Where to buy" links if you have them.
4. Press **Publish changes**.

Drag books in the list to change the order they appear on the Books page.

---

## Adding an upcoming event

1. Click **Add an upcoming event**.
2. Fill in the title, the date, and where it is.
3. Press **Publish changes**.

An event disappears from the website by itself once its date has passed.

---

## In the Press

**Edit In the Press** lets you change the quotes shown on your Home page. Open a quote, change the words or who said it, and press **Save**. It goes live straight away. You edit the quotes that are there; you don't add or delete them (ask your developer if you'd like a new one added).

---

## Replacing a picture

1. Open the page, essay or book that has the picture.
2. Next to the picture, click **Remove**.
3. Click **Add new** and choose a photo from your computer, or **Choose from library** to reuse one you've uploaded before.
4. In the **Caption** box, type a short description of the picture. It's shown under the picture on essays, and it's read aloud to people who can't see the picture.
5. Press **Publish changes**.

**Picture tips**
- JPG, PNG and WebP photos all work.
- Very large photos (over about 10 MB) make the page slower. If you can, use a smaller copy (about 2000 pixels wide).

---

## Previewing before you publish

- Pages, essays, books and events show the preview beside the editor automatically.
- To see the page at phone size, use the size menu above the preview.
- To hide the preview and get more room to type, click the **eye** icon at the top.

---

## If you make a mistake

- **Not published yet?** Click the **⋯** menu next to **Publish changes** and choose **Discard unpublished changes**. It goes back to what's live.
- **Already published?** Open it and click **History**. Pick an earlier version and click **Bring back this version**, then publish.

Nothing you do in the editor can break the design of the site. Layout, colours and fonts are built in. You only ever change the words and pictures.

---

## Your account

Click your picture (top right) to change your name, email or password. If you forget your password, ask your developer to reset it.

---

## Getting help

If something looks wrong on the website after you publish, open **History** on that page and bring back the last good version. Then get in touch with your developer and tell them which page and roughly what time.
